How to play Beastgate Defense v7

Modes

  • Green Circle — demons run laps forever. You lose when the living count goes over the cap (Easy 120 · Normal 90 · Hard 75 · Nightmare 60). Waves come every 38s (30s after wave 10) whether you are ready or not — vote CALL WAVE to send one early for bonus gold. Survive 20 waves, then purge the circle within 5 minutes.
  • Soul Crystal — your Crystal sits in the center of the map, ringed by the Crypt Ring road. Enemies pour out of 3 Hellgates around the edges, wind toward the ring, then circle it (clockwise or counter-clockwise) before turning in at one of 4 spokes. A new gate tears open every 5 waves — Gate IV at wave 5, V at 10, VI at 15 and VII at 20 (7 gates total). Each gate has a bone shortcut straight to the Crystal that opens on wave 4 — fast enemies race through it. 25 waves. After each cleared wave a countdown starts (Easy 60s · Normal 50s · Hard 40s · Nightmare 30s); press READY to skip it for bonus gold.
  • Every 5th wave is a boss wave: the boss arrives with 2 Sub-Bosses (huge elite champions with a purple aura). Bosses are now bigger and much tougher.

Controls

  • WASD/arrows or right-click move · wheel zoom · C free camera · minimap: click to look, right-click to move
  • Click a summoning circle → 1–9, 0, - summon one of 13 monsters (= Reaper, [ Kraken) (your hero walks over if too far)
  • U evolve · F mend · T targeting · X release (70%) · Q/R hero skills at the mouse · G ping · B recipe book · Y/N accept/decline fusion · Space ready/call wave · Tab damage meter · P pause (host) · M mute

Evolution — 5 levels

Hatchling → Juvenile → Elder (skill empowered) → Ancient → APEX (new form, skill transforms). Each level grows the monster, changes its body, glow and attacks.

Monster towers

    ⚗ Monster Fusion

    Two Ancient/APEX monsters next to each other can fuse into one of 12 fusion monsters (300g). Open the recipe book with B. Unknown recipes show as ??? until someone discovers them.

    ⚡ Duo Strikes

    When two heroes cast Q within 2 seconds of each other while standing close (about 300 px when they cast), they unleash a joint attack: Frostfire Slash (Kael+Seren), Storm Volley (Brakk+Nyx), Thunderblade (Kael+Brakk), Frost Rain (Nyx+Seren), Shadow Flame (Kael+Nyx), Glacier Hammer (Brakk+Seren), Pyre of Judgment (Kael+Grimm), Night Execution (Nyx+Grimm), Thunder Guillotine (Brakk+Grimm), Frozen Sentence (Seren+Grimm), Twin Strike (same hero). A "DUO!" prompt appears when a teammate just used Q near you.

    ☠ Heroes can fall

    Enemies that touch your hero, tower-wreckers and bosses now hurt heroes. A fallen hero is down for 10 seconds — a teammate standing beside them revives them in 3 seconds. Heroes slowly heal when no enemy is near.

    🧬 The horde adapts

    Every 5 waves (Soul Crystal: waves 6, 11, 16 and 21) enemies gain +10% resistance to the damage type (physical or magic) that dealt over 60% of the damage. Keep your mix balanced and they cannot adapt!

    🤝 Teamwork bosses

    • Butcher Ogre — its hook pulls heroes. If another hero stands next to the target, the hook SNAPS and the Ogre is stunned.
    • Twin Wraith Queens — when one dies, kill the other within 5 seconds or the fallen Queen rises again.
    • Ashen Dragon Lord — in phase 4 it raises a WARD (70% damage reduction). Two heroes must hit it at the same moment (solo: your hero) — or land a Duo Strike — to break it.

    🔥 Early pressure

    • Waves 1–5 have up to +40% health (fading out by wave 6), every enemy is 15% faster, and groups arrive 20% closer together. Easy halves these boosts. Playing solo? Your monsters deal +20% damage (Lone Warden bonus) since you have no Duo Strikes.
    • Elites (Haste or Regen) appear from wave 2. Skull Brutes smash towers from wave 3.
    • Pack Leaders (red aura, bigger, ☠) lead waves 1–4. Nearby enemies run 15% faster — kill the leader first.
    • More heroes = a bigger horde: more enemies per extra player — Green Circle +40% (4p ×2.2), Soul Crystal +45% (4p ×2.35), with only +10% health per player.

    Combos

    • SHATTER — frozen enemies take +50% from Magma and Ballista.
    • CONDUCT — enemies in acid take +25% lightning damage.
    • BLOOD PACT — cursed enemies dying near a Lich raise an extra skeleton.
    • MOLTEN — Magma ignores 50% armor on enemies cracked by the Ironshell.

    Enemies

    • Shieldbearer Paladins shield allies — kill them first. Silencer Hags block tower skills near them. Brood Mothers burst into 8 spiderlings. Siege Rams hunt towers and are armored from the front — hit them from behind.
    • Elites (glowing ring): Haste », Regen +, Bone Shield ◈, Blink ✦, Frost-immune ❄, Berserk !, Splitter ⑂, Vampiric ♥. From wave 12 they stack 2–3 powers.
    • Relics: after waves 5, 10 and 15 (Crystal: 5, 10, 15, 20) every hero picks 1 of 3 relics.

    LAN tips

    • Same Wi-Fi/router, Chrome or Edge. Can't connect? Allow the browser in Windows Firewall (Private), avoid guest/office Wi-Fi, turn off VPNs.
    • Test on one PC first: open the file in two windows — one hosts, one joins.

    📖 Fusion Recipe Book

    Select an Ancient or APEX monster. If a fusion partner stands within range, a ⚗ Fuse button appears (300g). Fusing frees one summoning circle. Fusing with a teammate's monster needs their approval. Each hero may own only one of each fused monster. Discoveries are remembered on this PC.
